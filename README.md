# Coffee Sales Dashboard Project

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Coffee Sales Dashboard built in Excel to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product trends, and country-wise sales distribution.

The dashboard provides dynamic filtering using slicers and visual insights to support business decision-making.

---

# Tools Used

*  Excel
* Pivot Tables
* Pivot Charts
* Slicers
* KPI Cards
* Data Visualization

---

# Dashboard Features

## Interactive Filters (Slicers)

The dashboard includes dynamic slicers for:

* Loyalty Card
* Coffee Type
* Coffee Size
* Order Date

These filters allow users to interactively explore the data and analyze specific segments.

# Business Questions & Insights

## 1. What is the total sales performance?

### Answer:

Total sales reached **$45,134**, indicating strong overall business performance.

---

## 2. Which country generated the highest sales?

### Answer:

The **United States** generated the highest sales with more than **$35K**, contributing approximately **77%** of total sales.

### Insight:

The business heavily depends on the U.S. market.

---

## 3. Which country had the lowest sales?

### Answer:

The **United Kingdom** recorded the lowest sales among all countries.

---

## 4. How did sales change over time?

### Answer:

Sales fluctuated over time with noticeable peaks during specific periods, indicating possible seasonal demand patterns.

---

## 5. Which coffee types performed best?

### Answer:

**Liberica** and **Excelsa** showed strong sales spikes during multiple periods.

---

## 6. Who are the top customers?

### Answer:

The highest customer generated more than **$317** in sales, highlighting the importance of loyal and high-value customers.

---

## 7. What is the average sales value?

### Answer:

The average sales value per order is approximately **$45.13**.

---

## 8. Does loyalty card usage impact sales?

### Answer:

Customers using loyalty cards positively contributed to sales performance, showing the effectiveness of customer retention strategies.

---

# Key Business Insight

The United States dominates coffee sales performance, contributing over 77% of total revenue, while customer loyalty and product variety significantly support sales growth.

# Conclusion

This dashboard helps transform raw coffee sales data into meaningful business insights through interactive visualizations and KPI analysis. It supports better decision-making by identifying sales trends, customer behavior, and market performance.
